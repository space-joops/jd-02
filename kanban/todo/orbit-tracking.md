# 🌍 Geolocation & Real-time Orbit Tracking

## 🎯 Design Vision
Space Joops isn't just a game; it's a virtual pet living in Low Earth Orbit (LEO). We want to give players a sense of "presence" by tying the pet's location to the real world.

## 📝 Specifications
- **Geo-location Check**: Request user's rough latitude/longitude.
- **Orbit Calculation**: Simulate the pet's orbital trajectory around Earth (90-minute orbital period).
- **Overhead Bonus**: When the pet passes over the player's real-world country/location, grant a massive temporary buff (e.g., "Home Territory Advantage: 2x JUNK, Infinite Fuel").
- **Visuals**: A small mini-map or radar on the Title Screen showing the pet's current coordinates.

## 🧠 Designer's Note
This feature bridges the gap between casual arcade and persistent idle games. Players will set alarms to play when their pet is "overhead."
