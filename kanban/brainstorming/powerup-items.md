# ⚡ Power-ups & Dynamic Hazards Design

## 💡 Brainstorming Ideas
- **The Shield (Power-up)**:
  - Spawns rarely (5%).
  - Grants 1 extra hit point that doesn't consume the invincibility frame.
  - Visual: A glowing hexagonal aura around the pet.
- **The EMP Bomb (Power-up)**:
  - Destroys all hazards currently on screen and converts them to 50 JUNK each.
- **Smart Mines (Hazard)**:
  - Instead of falling straight down, they slowly track the player's position.
  - Forces the player to use high-thrust maneuvers to escape.

## 🧠 Designer's Note
Right now, the game relies entirely on density for difficulty. Smart tracking hazards will force players to utilize the momentum mechanics more skillfully, elevating the skill ceiling without alienating casual players.
