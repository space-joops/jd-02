# 🛒 Economy & Meta-Progression (JUNK Shop)

## 🎯 Design Vision
To keep players coming back, we needed a meta-progression system. A single run shouldn't feel like a waste if you die; you must carry over value.

## ✅ Completed Features
- **Persistent Currency**: JUNK collected during gameplay is saved to `localStorage`.
- **Upgrades**:
  - `FUEL TANK`: Increases maximum thrust duration.
  - `THRUSTER`: Increases movement speed per thrust.
  - `MAGNET`: Increases the auto-attract radius for nearby junk.
- **HUD Integration**: Upgrades are displayed beautifully on the Title Screen, giving players immediate short-term goals before they press "TAP TO START".

## 🧠 Designer's Note
By capping upgrades at Level 5, we give players an achievable ceiling for the MVP. Next, we need to introduce 'prestiging' or infinite scaling.
